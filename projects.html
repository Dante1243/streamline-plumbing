Apartments & Multi-Residential

Streamline Plumbing has been involved in major mixed developments, such as a multi-level mixed-use retail and residential apartment complex located over basement car parks.

We have also been involved in the latest developments with renewable energy and sustainable water initiatives within strata residential projects, as well as integrated stormwater management, rainwater harvesting and water efficient fixtures. The apartments have water tanks to store rainwater, which also irrigate landscaping and communal gardens.

Recent projects

  • Evermore Apartments, Fremantle
  • Rialto Apartments, Fremantle
  • Rose on Angove, North Perth
