Occupational health & safety

Safety on every site

Management, staff and subcontractors strongly promote safety in the workplace and the implementation of our OHS commitments.

Our commitment

A safe and healthy work environment

As part of our commitment to the principles of health and safety in our workplace, we recognise our moral and legal responsibility to provide a safe and healthy work environment for workers, contractors, customers and visitors.

This commitment also extends to making sure our operations and activities do not put the local community or environment at risk of injury, illness or damage.

Aims & objectives

We will adopt procedures to:

  • Provide and maintain healthy and safe workplaces, safe plant and systems of work.
  • Provide written procedures and instructions to ensure safe work practices.
  • Ensure compliance with legislative duties and current industry standards.
  • Provide the information, instruction, training and supervision workers, contractors and customers need for their continued health and safety.
  • Provide support and assistance to workers and involve them in consultation on safety issues.
Responsibilities

Everyone has a part to play

We recognise that the overall responsibility to provide a safe workplace rests with management, who are accountable for implementing this policy.

Management

  • Ensuring that all OHS policies and procedures are implemented.
  • Establishing measurable objectives and targets for continued improvement, aimed at eliminating work-related injuries and illnesses.
  • Providing adequate resources to meet these OHS commitments.

Workers

  • Following all OHS policies and procedures.
  • Recognising hazards which may affect the health and safety of themselves, others or the environment.
