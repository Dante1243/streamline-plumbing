About us

A team built for projects of any size

Streamline Plumbing employs a range of qualified personnel, including plumbers, apprentices, plant operators and front office staff, who offer expertise in plumbing projects varying in size and complexity.

We are committed to developing and training apprentices. Over the years, we have been a part of many apprentices’ journeys to becoming fully qualified plumbers.

We have also invested in courses to further train our existing team. In addition to our current qualified personnel, Streamline Plumbing has access to labour hire recruiting companies to assist with projects when required.

Training & apprenticeship

Training the next generation of plumbers

Streamline Plumbing has always made sure we have apprentices joining our team.

On and off the job

We pride ourselves on giving apprentices a high standard of on and off the job training through MPA Skills and other registered training organisations, so they get the best possible trade education.

Host employer

We have supported MPA Skills and Apprentice Training since they were established. Streamline is also a ‘Host Employer’ of plumbing apprentices and the Training Group.

School-based training

We assist with school-based training, including on the job training for pre-apprentices on block release.
